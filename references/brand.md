# SolScape Retreats — Brand

> Source of truth for who SolScape is. Read before any design or copy work.

## In one line

Women's wellness retreats built around **Movement · Travel · Connection** — yoga, barre, and
intentional movement, held in beautiful places, for women who want to slow down and reconnect.

## Positioning

- **Movement** = the core. Yoga *and* barre/ballet-based movement, plus breathwork, sound
  healing, and nature. Not a generic "yoga retreat" — movement in the fullest sense.
- **Travel** = real destinations with a sense of place (Mediterranean roots, expanding wider).
- **Connection** = small, intimate cohorts. The group becomes friends by day two.
- The feeling they sell: *"Nervous system reset. This is where body, mind, and soul come home."*
- Stage: early, founder-led, ~3 years running. Trust and beauty matter more than scale.

## Audience

Women seeking a restorative-but-active retreat — not a party, not a silent meditation camp.
They value taste, intention, and being *cared for*. The site speaks **to her, directly.**

They built SolScape because they couldn't find the retreat they wanted, so they made it.

## Destinations

- **Run so far:** Egypt, Greece, Tuscany, Spain (past 3 years).
- **Upcoming (current site):** Greece (Oct 2026), Kenya (Nov 2026), Egypt/Nile (Jan 2027),
  Morocco (Feb 2027), Provence (Apr 2027), Geneva (Jul 2027), plus an "Exotic — TBA" teaser.
- Each retreat carries a one-word motif: *exhale, wander, rooted, reflection, expansive…*
  (Keep these lowercase and consistent — the old site had a stray "SoFen" placeholder and mixed
  capitalization. Clean that up.)

## Voice & Tone

Soulful, warm, and declarative. Second-person and intimate. Editorial, never clinical, never
cliché (no "namaste," no hashtag-wellness speak).

**On-brand phrases (real, from their own materials):**
- "We slow down on purpose."
- "Eight ways to come alive."
- "Where will you reset?"
- "Movement & Meaning, designed just for you."
- "A retreat made for your soul."
- "move · explore · reconnect"
- "Empowered. Connected. Limitless."

**Avoid:** corporate wellness jargon, exclamation-heavy hype, generic luxury-travel filler.

## Booking model

Inquiry-based. No checkout. Every CTA → Instagram DM (`ig.me/m/solscape_retreats`) and email
(`solscaperetreats@gmail.com`). Newsletter capture + a Netlify contact form support this.

## Values (use on About / give-back sections)

Movement · Community · Sustainability. Small groups, lighter footprint, respect for place.
A give-back commitment (reforestation/conservation) reinforces credibility.

## The standard ("not normal / exceeds expectations")

The danger is the generic warm-wellness template: beige, one serif headline, a big photo, done.
SolScape's actual Instagram is already tasteful — so the website has to be *more* crafted than
that, not less. Express the brand through: editorial layout, a genuine sense of place per
destination, expressive typography, and **slow, breathing motion** ("we slow down on purpose"
as a literal design principle). See `design-system.md`.
